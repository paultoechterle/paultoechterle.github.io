Profile

Hydrogeologist focusing on alpine water and energy systems. My work combines fieldwork with statistical analysis and numerical modelling, through to implementation in spatial planning. Alongside my professional work, I develop Python-based calculation and analysis tools that turn methods from geology and hydrology into reproducible, practice-oriented workflows.
